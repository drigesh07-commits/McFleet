How to join

Pick your device

Java and Bedrock players share one world. The address is always McFleet.lol.

💻

Java

  1. Open Minecraft Java and choose Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server.
  3. Enter McFleet.lol as the address and join.
📱

Phone & tablet

  1. Open Minecraft and tap Play, then Servers.
  2. Choose Add Server.
  3. Address McFleet.lol, port 19132.
🎮

Console

  1. Join our Discord.
  2. Ask staff in the help channel.
  3. They’ll walk you through joining on your console.

Lifesteal basics

McFleet is a Lifesteal SMP. Every kill adds a heart to you and takes one from your rival, so winning fights makes you stronger and losing them makes you weaker.

Getting more hearts

Besides winning fights, you can pick up hearts from the store. Choose the Hearts category, add a pack to your basket, then finish the order through a Discord ticket.

Joining on Java and Bedrock

Java and Bedrock players share one world. The address is always McFleet.lol, and Bedrock uses port 19132. Full steps are in the How to join section on the home page.

Getting help

Open a ticket in the McFleet Discord for reports, appeals, bugs and store problems. Check the rulebook first if you are unsure about something.
